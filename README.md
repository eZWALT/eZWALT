### Walter J. Troiani Vargas

AI infrastructure engineer. The interesting problems are under the model: how it is compiled, scheduled, stored, and served.

Founding AI Infrastructure Engineer at [Speridlabs](https://speridlabs.com), a spatial AI lab in San Francisco.

Before that: GPU platforms and MLOps at Multiverse Computing, LLVM and OpenMP at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, research computing at CERN. Master's thesis with Telefónica on what advertising costs inside an LLM conversation, measured in behaviour and EEG. Double MSc in Data Science, Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya and Università degli Studi di Padova.
